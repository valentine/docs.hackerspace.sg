## Event Organisers {#organiser}

HackerspaceSG welcomes a wide range of events, particularly in the areas of:

- Hacking[^1] and Technology
- Entrepreneurship
- Interesting/unusual/offbeat thinkers
- After-parties for events in the above areas held elsewhere

The standard price for events is **$256 SGD** for a 4-hour block, any time between 9 AM to 12 midnight. Events outside these hours may incur a surcharge. [Members](membership) may get up to 100% discount off the standard price.

Our main event room can fit around 20 people comfortably in theatre seating format.

### Minimum Attendance

Events hosted by **supporters** require at least **4 supporters or members** in attendance (including the host). Members can host events on their own, as has always been the case.

All events must comply with Singapore law.[^2]

### Booking The Space

To book the space, email events@hackerspace.sg.

### Membership Discount

To enjoy a membership discount, the event must fall within the following guidelines:

- Be mostly open to the public
- Be of interest to any of the tech, design, hacker, or maker communities
- Be not-for-profit
- For more than 5 people: be outside office hours (after 6 PM on weekdays, all day on Saturday/Sunday)
- Ticket sales at token prices (<$10) to ensure attendance are generally allowed

We aim to apply the full 100% membership discount to most events that fall within our interests and the above guidelines.

If your event is part of, or is sponsored by a large organisation with an event or marketing budget, we may ask to invoice the organisation to cover our operational costs.

## Hosting Member {#host}

Whether you're hosting your own event or someone else's, you're taking on several specific responsibilities:

- Prepare the space before the event
- Be present throughout as host, taking responsibility for non-member attendees as your guests
- Clean up the space afterwards

Don't shirk these. Putting other members in the position of having to take over because you didn't show up is bad karma. Only agree to host events you actually want to attend.

### Booking an event

- Find a free spot on the [events calendar](https://hackerspace.sg/events) at least a week out (preferably more)
- Email the [events officers](mailto:events@hackerspace.sg) with:
  1. Event title
  2. Preferred date, time, and duration
  3. Description (include links to Meetup/Facebook event pages if they exist)
  4. Confirmation that you'll act as host
  5. Your mobile number for use during the event[^3]
- Once confirmed, invite your attendees (or tell the organisers to go ahead)

### On the day

**Setup (arrive 30 minutes early):**
- Chock the door ajar
- Turn on all air-conditioners — 24°C, auto fan, oscillation. 30 minutes is enough to cool the space without extreme settings.
- Arrange furniture as needed

**During the event:**
- Welcome guests as they arrive
- When you're ready to start, welcome everyone to HackerspaceSG. If we're sponsoring the venue, invite guests to make use of our drinks supply and make contributions. Something like:

  > Welcome to HackerspaceSG! We're a not-for-profit, member-funded community space for the technology and maker community in Singapore. We host events like this one, and we provide a space for people to hack, build, and hang out.
  >
  > HackerspaceSG is the venue sponsor for this event. If you like what we're doing — especially if you'd like to host events here yourself — consider becoming a [supporter](https://hackerspace.sg/support) or a [member](https://hackerspace.sg/membership).
  >
  > We aren't licensed to sell food and beverages, but we make our drinks available to visitors and encourage donations — suggested amounts are on the tip jars near the fridge.

**Wrap-up:**
- Return all furniture to its usual location
- Feed and start the dishwasher (instructions on the pantry cupboard)
- Remain until all non-member attendees have left
- Empty all garbage bins
- Unchock the door
- If no members remain, turn off air-conditioning and lights

## Event Officers {#officer}

Before adding an event to the calendar, ensure a specific member has agreed to host:

- Don't accept requests from someone other than the hosting member, even if they say "member X said he would host." X may not have agreed, or may not know what's involved.
- Ensure the host understands the responsibilities above and is willing to carry them out — at minimum, being present throughout plus setup and teardown time.
- Unless you're personally willing to host, advise non-member organisers to find a host first. Don't accept bookings with no host and then ask the mailing list for a volunteer.

For not-for-profit community events, err on the side of inclusiveness. If an event is even remotely related to the areas listed above, accept it at the full membership discount, subject to calendar availability.

- If there are concerns about legality, discuss with HackerspaceSG's directors.
- If there are concerns about an event falling outside guidelines or being potentially objectionable, discuss on any of our [channels](connect) (preferably Telegram) before committing a calendar slot.
- HackerspaceSG reserves the right to decline event requests without explanation.

If the organisers haven't created a Facebook or Meetup event page, offer to create one on the [Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/HackerspaceSG) or the [Meetup.com group](https://www.meetup.com/hackerspace-sg/). Include the link in the calendar entry.

[^1]: "[Hacking](http://www.catb.org/jargon/html/H/hacker.html)" meaning ingenuity or playful cleverness, not criminal activity.

[^2]: "Within the law" should go without saying, but occasionally people are confused about the term "hacker".

[^3]: If you're not willing to share your number with event attendees, please reconsider being an event host — it's important for guests to be able to reach you if you're running late.
