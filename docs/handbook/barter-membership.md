HackerspaceSG is a self-managed community, and Barter Members help keep the space running, welcoming, and accessible to more people. In return, Barter Members get **24/7 access** to the space (the same as a [Co-working membership](/handbook/membership)).[^1]

This is a barter arrangement, not volunteering. You're trading time and effort for access — a fair exchange with expectations on both sides. We ask you to do specific things during your shifts, and in return you get full use of the space. If that sounds good, read on.

:::info Why "Barter Membership" instead of "Volunteer"?

The word "volunteer" implies charity — they're doing you a favour, so you can't direct them. "Barter Membership" makes the exchange explicit: time and effort for access. It's a fair deal with expectations on both sides.

:::

One of our goals is to make HackerspaceSG more accessible, especially for people who want to be part of the community but can't afford a membership. Barter Membership is one way to trade time for access.

We're looking for **reliable** and **responsible** people who want to give back, be involved, and be a net positive to the space — helping in small or big ways to keep it friendly, tidy, and running smoothly.

## The Real Goal: Making HSG Part of Your Week

The Barter Membership isn't just about filling shifts. It's about building a habit — making HackerspaceSG a regular part of your weekly routine, the way a gym or a favourite café becomes part of your life.

The Barter Members who thrive here are the ones who stop thinking of it as "I have a shift on Tuesday" and start thinking of it as "Tuesday evening is my HSG evening." You come in, you do your thing, you keep an eye on the space, you chat with whoever's around. It becomes your third place.

**What makes this work:**
- **Pick a consistent schedule.** Same days, same times, every week. Routine builds habit. Habit builds belonging.
- **Treat the space like your living room.** Not "I'm on duty" — more like "I'm home, and if someone rings the doorbell, I'll get it."
- **Bring your own work.** Shifts are relaxed — bring your laptop, work on personal projects, study, tinker. The point is to be present and available, not busy the whole time.
- **Get to know the regulars.** The people who show up on the same evenings as you will become your HSG circle. That's the kampong forming.

The first month is the hardest. After that, it's just where you go on Tuesday.

## Two Barter Roles

- **Host** — keep the space welcoming and be the point of contact
- **Maker** — keep the hardware areas tidy, safe, and usable

## 🙋 What's Involved (Both Roles)

- **Commit at least 2 months**
  - It takes a while to get familiar with the space and the people
  - We're flexible — just let us know about exams, travel, or life stuff ahead of time
  - We'll use the first 1–2 weeks as a supervised trial to see if it works for both you and us
- **Pick a consistent weekly schedule and stick to it**
  - Use the shared Google Calendar
  - Please pick times that complement other Barter Members
  - If possible, try to choose shift times that match visitor bookings (like day pass users or events)
  - Consistency matters more than total hours — showing up reliably on the same days builds trust and routine
- **Be responsive to direction**
  - This is a barter arrangement. We may ask you to do specific things during your shifts — greeting visitors, tidying a particular area, helping with a task. That's part of the deal.
  - If something comes up that needs doing and you're on shift, it's yours.
- **Use Telegram**
  - Barter Members should be on Telegram, and generally responsive — just let us know if you'll be <abbr title="Away from keyboard">AFK</abbr> for a while
  - Stay available on Telegram while on shift
- **Report issues**
  - Small issues (e.g. something needs restocking, or is missing an owner): post in the appropriate Telegram group
  - Larger issues: open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/hackerspacesg/admin/issues)
- **Walk through the space** when you start and end your shift, and put things where they should be
  - Just a quick check to see if anything looks off or needs attention
  - As a self-managed space, we experience our fair share of chaos, entropy, and stagnancy — your help keeps things nice and usable for everyone
  - All members and visitors should clean up after themselves[^2]
- **Be a net positive**
  - Be respectful, helpful, and kind
  - Passion is great, but humility keeps things welcoming — try to stay open to other ways of doing things

## 👥 Host (Barter)

Hosts help make HackerspaceSG feel welcoming. They greet people, answer questions, and act as a friendly point of contact during open hours.

### What You'll Do

- Act as the main point of contact for people looking to visit the space
- Welcome and check in co-working desk pass users, and give them a quick orientation if needed
- Help with other co-working bookings and drop-ins (like from Switch, a co-working marketplace)

Most of the time, you'll just be hanging out — the key is to be around if someone rings the doorbell or needs help.

### Time Commitment

- **16 hours** per week, in 4-hour blocks, any time between 10am to midnight

## 🔧 Maker (Barter)

Makers help keep the hardware areas tidy, safe, and usable. They focus on tools, not on projects.

### What You'll Do

- Keep shared tools in working order, and the Hardware Room organised
- Supervise and assist visitors who buy a hardware room day pass
- Show members and other participants how to safely use tools
- Be able to independently set up and operate the Anycubic i3 Mega 3D printer
- Help with occasional maker events or hardware hack sessions

### Time Commitment

- **8 hours** per week, in 4-hour blocks (weekday evenings or weekends)

Feel free to work on your own stuff — just make sure tools are cared for and the space stays safe.

## 🎟️ Who This Is For

This is great for:

- Students, retirees, or people in between jobs or on sabbatical
- Anyone who'd prefer to trade time for space access
- Makers and tinkerers who want to stay involved even if they're not currently paying members
- Anyone who likes helping out, being hands-on, or just hanging around HackerspaceSG
- People looking for a low-stress, chill way to stay involved — it's not a second job
- People who want a regular "third place" in their week — somewhere that isn't home or work

Barter Members get:

- 24/7 access to HackerspaceSG (same as a Co-working member)[^1]
- A chance to be more involved in the community
- A weekly routine that puts you in the middle of Singapore's oldest hackerspace

## 🔄 The Barter-to-Community Pipeline

Barter Membership is often how people go from "I've heard of HackerspaceSG" to "this is my place." The progression looks like:

1. **Month 1:** You're learning the space, the tools, the people. It feels new.
2. **Month 2–3:** You've got your routine. You know the regulars. You have your spot.
3. **Month 4+:** You're part of the furniture. People ask you questions. You have opinions about the space. You might start thinking about becoming a paying member or supporter.

Not everyone follows this path, and that's fine. But the Barter Members who stick around tend to become some of HSG's most committed community members — because they built the habit first.

## ✍️ How to Apply

1. Message **HackerspaceSG Admin** on Telegram
2. Tell us a bit about yourself and your availability
3. We'll arrange a quick **in-person chat**
4. If it's a good fit, we'll onboard you and give you access to the shared calendar

[^1]: Unlimited access means you can come and go any time, _up to_ 16 hours a day. You can't live out of the space — though we get it if there's an occasional deadline.

[^2]: Members are expected to clean up after themselves. Barter Members aren't cleaners — you're not expected to tidy up after others, and are empowered to remind other members to do their part. Basic space citizenship (clean up, answer the door, be welcoming) is every member's responsibility — not just yours.
